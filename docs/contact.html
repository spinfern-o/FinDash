Contact FinDash

The quickest way to reach FinDash is email: support.findash@findash.tech. Every kind of question goes to that one address.

What to write about

Help with your figures
A spreadsheet that won't read, a total that looks wrong, or a term you'd like explained. The help page may already have the answer.
Problems with the site
Something broken, a page that won't load, or a link that goes nowhere: report a problem.
Your account and your data
Logging in, deleting your account, or getting a copy of what we hold about you. You can delete your account yourself from the log in page; the privacy policy explains your other rights.
Security
If you think you've found a security problem, please email the details before sharing them anywhere else.
Ideas, partnerships and press
Suggestions for what FinDash should do next, and anything else: say hello.

What to include

  • The page you were on, and what you did just before the problem.
  • What you expected to see, and what you saw instead. A screenshot helps.
  • The browser and device you were using.

Please don't email passwords, bank details or card numbers. If a problem only shows with your own spreadsheet, say so and we'll ask before you send anything.
