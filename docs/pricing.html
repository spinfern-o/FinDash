Pricing

The dashboard is free for everyone, with or without an account. FinDash Plus is for owners who add their supplier invoices by photo every week: $19 a month, or $190 a year.

Why only some things are paid

Everything that happens in your own browser costs us almost nothing, so it stays free and unlimited: reading your spreadsheet, every card on the dashboard, the forecast, export and print. Reading an invoice photo and writing advice are done by Claude, an AI model, and every request costs us money. Plus pays for enough of them to keep up with a business's deliveries.

Paying, and cancelling

  • Get Plus from the account page once you're logged in. You pay on Stripe's secure checkout page; FinDash never sees your card.
  • It renews each month or year until you cancel, and Stripe emails you a receipt each time.
  • Cancel any time with Manage billing on the account page. Plus carries on until the end of the period you've paid for, then you're back on the free plan. Your figures stay.
  • Deleting your account ends Plus at once, and you aren't charged again.
  • Prices are in US dollars. Your bank may convert them. Where sales tax applies, the checkout page shows it before you pay.

The terms have the details. Questions about a payment: support.findash@findash.tech.
